Plainly

An estimate, not a fixed fare
Your final fare follows the actual trip. Added stops, waiting, a route change, weather, or traffic can change it.
The airport pickup fee
A $15 airport pickup fee applies to trips originating at the airport. This helps cover the additional time and coordination involved in arranging your pickup and meeting you at the designated pickup location. The fee is included in your displayed total before booking.
It is Bridger Reserve's own fee, not a charge from the airport. We track your flight number, so your pickup follows your actual arrival.
Waiting
Each pickup and each stop you ask for includes 5 minutes of waiting, or 15 at an airport pickup. After that, waiting is $1.30 a minute, counted by the second. The regular trip clock pauses while we wait, so you are never charged twice. Traffic lights and slow traffic are never billed as waiting.
Deposit and cancellation
A 25% deposit, rounded up to the nearest $5, confirms your booking. It applies toward your total fare. It is not an extra charge. For a $172.25 trip, the deposit is $45.00 and $127.25 remains.
If you cancel, the deposit is kept and not refunded.
Not in the estimate
Gratuity and tax are not added. Travel to a distant pickup, or an empty return, is quoted before you confirm, when it applies.
