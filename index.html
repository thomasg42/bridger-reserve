The fare

Read it first.

Four rates make the trip. Four more apply only when they happen.

  1. $20 Base fare Once per booking.
  2. $0.95 A minute of driving Driving time on the passenger trip.
  3. $1.50 A mile Passenger trip distance.
  4. $15 Airport pickup fee, Bridger Reserve Only when the pickup is at BZN. Drop-off at BZN is $0.

When they happen

Waiting at a scheduled pickup
The first 5 minutes are included. Then $1.30 a minute.
A stop you ask for
$1.30 a minute from the first minute.
Slow traffic, below 10 mph, including snow
$1.30 a minute in place of the $0.95 driving rate. Miles continue.
Empty travel
The drive to a distant pickup, or an empty return, quoted when it applies. Same $0.95 and $1.50. No second base fare. No second airport fee.

Waiting, stop, and slow-traffic minutes use $1.30 a minute in place of the $0.95 driving rate. They never stack on it.
